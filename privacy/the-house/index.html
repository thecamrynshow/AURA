THE HOUSE is a single-player strategy game. Fictional currency only. No cash-out. No real-money wagering. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

Who we are

THE HOUSE is published by PNEUOMA. Contact: camrynjackson@pneuoma.com.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not create an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not include advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

In-game “analytics” exist only in memory on your device while you play. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Progress and settings are stored locally on your device, including:

  • Your save (nights, ledger, table, named people, settings)
  • Sound and haptics preferences

Deleting the app deletes that save. We cannot recover it. We cannot read it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone
  • No advertising identifiers, IDFA, or App Tracking Transparency prompts
  • No sale or sharing of personal data
  • No chip packs, daily wheels, or real-money paths

Purchases

If you buy THE HOUSE on the App Store, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your credit card number. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles App Store purchases.

Children

THE HOUSE is a casino-set strategy game with simulated gambling. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no profile on our servers to access, export, or delete. To stop local processing, delete the app from your device.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California and have a request, email camrynjackson@pneuoma.com. We will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes that would involve collecting personal information will be reflected here before that collection begins.

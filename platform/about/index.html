About PNEUOMA

πνεῦμα — Greek for "breath, spirit, soul"

Our Mission

To create intelligent, human-centered technologies that improve how people learn, work, perform, connect, and experience the world.

PNEUOMA develops intelligent software, artificial intelligence, interactive experiences, and human-centered systems. Regulation technology is one of four disciplines, alongside software, artificial intelligence, and games. The products are separate. A regulation game is not an enterprise deployment, and a research module is not a treatment.

What the company builds

Software for school operations, interactive games, creative tools, and research prototypes. Custom enterprise software and private AI are services an organization can ask PNEUOMA to scope. They are not a list of completed deployments.

Our Values

Make the real thing

Show the product that exists. Do not present a concept as a deployment.

Separate the work

Software, games, research, and regulation tools each have their own audience. One product does not prove the others.

Open where it can be

Many experiences run in the browser. Sign-in products say so on the page.

Careful with personal data

Inquiry forms ask people not to send student records, health information, or credentials. A privacy page covers the products that collect data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

PNEUOMA Capture ("the App") is developed and operated by PNEUOMA ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Voice Recordings: When you use the voice capture feature, audio is recorded on your device and transmitted to our server for transcription. Audio is processed in real time and is not permanently stored on our servers after transcription is complete.

Incident Data: Reports you create — including descriptions, timestamps, locations, people involved, and actions taken — are stored on our servers to provide the service. You control what information is entered.

Identity Labels: The App provides name anonymization by default (e.g., "Person A," "Person B"). Real names, when entered, are stored privately and only displayed when the recorder explicitly chooses to reveal them.

Device Information: We may collect basic device information (device type, operating system version) for troubleshooting and service improvement.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the App experience. This does not include the content of your reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To transcribe voice recordings into structured text using AI (OpenAI Whisper and GPT-4o)
  • To generate communication templates based on your report data
  • To store and retrieve your reports so you can access, edit, and export them
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide core functionality:

  • OpenAI: For voice transcription (Whisper) and AI-powered text structuring (GPT-4o). Audio and text are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's data usage policies apply. We use the API in a way that does not allow OpenAI to train on your data.
  • Render: For server hosting and data storage.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your report data is stored on secure servers hosted by Render. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

Your reports are retained as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete individual reports at any time from within the App. Voice recordings are processed in real time and are not retained after transcription.

6. Children's Privacy (FERPA Compliance)

The App is designed for use by adult administrators, managers, and professionals — not by children. When used in educational settings, the App includes built-in name anonymization to protect student identity by default, supporting compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the data stored in your reports
  • Delete individual reports or request deletion of all your data
  • Control how names are displayed in your reports (anonymized, real, initials, or custom)
  • Export your data as PDF reports at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Executive summary

PNEUOMA’s **Classroom Sync** was piloted in a live kindergarten classroom during daily transitions. Students participated consistently, reported feeling calmer and better afterward, and learned a standardized entry cue (“Reset in 3…2…1…”).

By **May 21**, the classroom teacher reported that Classroom Sync is **highly effective**, that she **loves it**, and that she **no longer needs the facilitator**—she will continue using it **daily during transitions** on her own. On **May 19**, she had already led a session before the facilitator arrived; students were actively doing the exercise when he walked in.

This pilot demonstrates:

1. **Feasibility** in real K–5 schedules (rain delays, subs, recess conflicts).
2. **Student engagement** without treating regulation as punishment.
3. **Teacher adoption** beyond a “visitor demo” model.
4. **Operational learnings** for scaling (timing, stacking exercises, facilitator boundaries).

## Protocol reference

Exercises used in Classroom Sync (durations from product config):

| Protocol | Duration | Pattern | Typical use (pilot) |
|----------|----------|---------|---------------------|
| **Breath Reset** | 2:00 | 4s in / 4s out | Early intro sessions |
| **Quick Ground** | 1:00 | 3s in / 5s out | Hyper, loud, scattered |
| **Transition** | 1:30 | 4s in / 6s out | Pre/post recess, context changes |
| **Focus Prep** | 2:00 | 4s in / hold 2s / 4s out | Pre-learning, focus block |
| **Calm Down** | 3:00 | 4s in / 8s out | Post-lunch rowdiness |

**Decision rule used in the field:**

- Hyper / loud / scattered → **Quick Ground** or **Calm Down**
- Sluggish / sleepy / unfocused → **Transition**
- Pre-work / need focus → **Focus Prep**

## What worked (repeat in future pilots)

### 1. Standard entry: “Reset in 3…2…1…”

Kids learned the cue and said it with the facilitator. Reduces banter and signals start time.

### 2. Wait outside until everyone is seated

Best sessions used: facilitator does **not** enter until class is seated and teacher is ready. Avoids “Mr. Jackson” social energy at the door.

### 3. One protocol per visit

Stacking (e.g. Focus Prep + Transition on 5/4) increased variability and second-session rowdiness. Single protocol supports repeatability and cleaner comparison data.

### 4. Match protocol to classroom state

Hyper → Ground/Calm Down; post-recess sluggish → Transition; pre-learning → Focus Prep.

### 5. Quick in, quick out

Kindergarten attention is finite. ~60–120 seconds of exercise + ~10 seconds of “how do you feel?” is enough.

### 6. Teacher ownership framing

On 5/7, students initially saw it as a “Mr. Jackson thing.” Teacher and facilitator reframed: **this is the class’s tool**; facilitator is only introducing it; goal is use **without** facilitator by next year / first grade.

### 7. Teacher boundary: hugs

Rule: hug facilitator only if he goes **outside** first—reduced distraction (5/12).

## Friction & mitigations

| Issue | What happened | Mitigation |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| Teacher talking during exercise | Corrections / hallway students distracted breath | Session norm: no other directions during 60–120s (see email template below) |
| Teacher debriefing behavior to facilitator | Facilitator correctly avoided discipline role | “I’ll catch you after—let’s reset now” |
| Slot timing | Kids not always in class at same time; teacher not always ready | 60s max wait; teacher signals ready; stragglers handled **before** start |
| Facilitator familiarity | Kids excited to see known adult | Fades with routine; teacher-led sessions prove transfer |
| Stacked exercises | Second session wilder | One protocol per visit |
| Schedule chaos | Rain, sub, recess, meetings | Expected in K–5; pilot still reached teacher adoption |

## Facilitator playbook (post-pilot)

### Session structure (locked for Week 2+)

| Slot | Recommended protocol |
|------|----------------------|
| Morning | Quick Ground |
| Pre-recess | Transition |
| Post-lunch recess | Transition or Calm Down |
| Pre-focus block | Focus Prep |

### Entry checklist

1. Arrive at slot; **wait outside** until all seated.
2. Enter → **“Reset in 3…2…1…”** (class joins).
3. Start **one** protocol immediately.
4. End → quick check (“thumbs up / how do you feel?”).
5. Exit cleanly; **no discipline conversations** during visit.

### Timing rule

- **Worth waiting:** up to **60 seconds** for seated, ready class.
- **Not worth waiting:** 2+ minutes—attention decays; late arrivals become the focus.

## Supplemental: 1:1 regulation (5/13)

When the class was at recess, one student stayed inside. Facilitator ran **grounding via Classroom Sync** plus a **touch-based regulation game** (gentle mouse movement attracts fish; fast movement scatters them—calmness as objective). Student reported feeling calm. Post-lunch sugar correlated with increased dysregulation afterward (contextual, not product failure).

**Implication:** Classroom Sync + individual games can support **counselor / para / indoor** use when whole-class session isn’t possible.

## Limitations (honest scope)

- **Informal outcomes** — no formal pre/post surveys or district incident data in this phase.
- **Single classroom** — one teacher, kindergarten, one school.
- **Facilitator effect** — early sessions influenced by students knowing the facilitator; mitigated by 5/19–5/21 teacher-led use.
- **Incomplete session count** — schedule disruptions are normal; adoption metric (teacher daily use) is the primary success signal for this phase.
